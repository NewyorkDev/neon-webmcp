## Intent

Show Booksy Reloaded as an independent WebMCP prototype inspired by my experience as a real Booksy customer. The video must prove the product works in the browser, not summarize it with abstract slides. It should show site-owned authentication, a configurable $50 automatic-booking limit, a structured exception when the usual provider is unavailable, an exact $46 pay-in-person match proceeding under standing permission, a confirmation preview, and the same appointment in the provider workspace.
