## Video direction

Neon's cream, ink, lime, lavender, and coral palette is used as flat editorial color blocks with square borders, one hard-offset feature block at most, and large display type. Motion uses smooth long-tail settles and reveals each new fact on its intended narration cue. Frames 4 and 6 contain deliberate held reads so the approval boundary and final provider proof remain legible. No gradients, blurred shadows, fake browser chrome, generic AI glows, breathing cards, screensaver motion, or front-loaded slideshow entrances. All important content stays above the bottom caption keep-out even though this cut is canonically silent.
